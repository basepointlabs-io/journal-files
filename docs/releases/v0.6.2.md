**Revit Doctor** watches the computer while Revit runs and explains Revit journal files in plain English. It works entirely on your computer: no AI, no uploads.

## What's new in 0.6.2

### Tidier layout
- **Two spacing sizes only, 8 and 16**, everywhere in the app: 16 around and between cards, tiles and sections, 8 inside them. Gaps that were uneven before (around the "Right now" card, between the tiles) are now the same.
- The "Right now" card fills the window with equal margins on both sides.
- Thin borders stay sharp on screens with fractional scaling (such as 125% or 175%); before, some of them blurred or disappeared.
- The status sign (check, warning, alarm) is centred against its title and text.
- In the Revit table the figures are centred in their cells.
- The disk tile is called **Free disk C:** and tile labels stay on one line, so the three figures line up.

### Tray overview
- It now grows **upwards** from the tray when a Revit or a problem appears, and shrinks back towards it, instead of running past the bottom of the screen.
- New buttons in its header: **open the full window** and **close** (Revit Doctor keeps watching from the tray).
- **What to do** shows each recommendation folded to its title; click it to read the advice. A recommendation you opened stays open while the figures update.

The version number now sits next to the name in the tray overview.

## Requirements

- Windows 10 or 11, x64 or ARM64. Nothing else to install (.NET is included).
- Reads Revit's journals in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Autodesk\Revit\Autodesk Revit 20XX\Journals`, the Windows Application event log (for crash proof), the computer's resource counters and the size of model files on this computer. Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Known limitations

- The new layout is checked with automated tests and rendered screens with sample figures; please report how it behaves during real work.
- RAM needed with linked models is an estimate: Autodesk does not say how linked models add to the 20× figure.
- Sizes of linked models are known only on the computer the journal comes from, and links stay counted until that Revit is closed.
- "RAM used" is the memory Revit has taken (private bytes); Task Manager may show a little less.
